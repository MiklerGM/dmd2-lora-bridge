Experimental. This release adds two ready-made boards with their own images: the **Heltec
WiFi LoRa 32 V4.3** and the **LILYGO T-Deck**. The firmware for EBYTE bridges is the same as in
v0.2.0, so an E220 or E22 bridge already flashed needs nothing.

Sideload the APK (it replaces the genuine DMD-LoRa app; they cannot coexist). It installs over
v0.2.1 without uninstalling, and the bridge stays paired. Flash the image for your board with
`esptool.py write_flash 0x0 <file>`:

| File | Board |
|---|---|
| `dmd2-lora-bridge-esp32dev.bin` | classic ESP32 wired to an EBYTE E220 or E22 |
| `dmd2-lora-bridge-esp32s3.bin` | ESP32-S3 wired to an EBYTE E220 or E22 |
| `dmd2-lora-bridge-esp32c3.bin` | ESP32-C3 wired to an EBYTE E220 or E22 |
| `dmd2-lora-bridge-heltec_sx1262.bin` | Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 **V4.3** only: earlier V4 boards have a different amplifier, and a V3 is untried |
| `dmd2-lora-bridge-tdeck_sx1262.bin` | LILYGO T-Deck / T-Deck Plus |

Flashing the full image clears the bridge's pairing, so pair it again in the app afterwards.

**If you build an EBYTE bridge: feed an E220-900T30 from 5 V**, not 3V3, or it transmits 20-30 dB
below its setting. See `docs/BUILDING.md`.

### What changed since v0.2.1

- **Heltec V4.3 and T-Deck images.** Both receive and transmit, and EBYTE modules take their
  packets: 29-30 of 30 across rooms on the bench. Neither has been ridden yet.
- **Their power levels** come from published numbers, not a meter of our own: the Heltec puts out
  28 / 24 / 19 / 14 dBm by Heltec's gain table for its KCT8103L amplifier (the table Meshtastic and
  RNode use), which our bench agreed with on average; the T-Deck 22 / 20 / 17 / 14 dBm at
  RadioLib's measured chip settings. Low on both is about 25 mW, the EU limit on this channel, and
  saves battery.
- **Signal bars for these bridges.** They report each packet's signal strength, the Heltec's
  corrected for its 21 dB receive amplifier.
- **App:** with the service off, the status screen shows the bridge in use and its radio, with
  Change to pick another. The bridge, its radio and its power share one line.

### Notes

- Measured on the bench, an EBYTE E22's four power steps span about 6 dB where its datasheet has
  12. The app shows the datasheet's numbers for E22 bridges.
- Transmit power: in the EU, 868.0-868.6 MHz allows 25 mW ERP, about 14 dBm. Every level of an
  E220-900T30 is above that, and so are all but Low on the Heltec and the T-Deck.
- After the app is updated or restarted, DMD2 may need the group shared again before the bridge
  transmits. The app says so: "Reshare the group in DMD2".
