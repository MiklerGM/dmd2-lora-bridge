**Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by DMD Navigation / Thork Racing.**
Experimental. This release changes the app only: the bridge firmware is the same as in v0.2.0,
so a bridge already flashed with v0.2.0 needs nothing. The images are attached as usual.

Sideload the APK (it replaces the genuine DMD-LoRa app; they cannot coexist). It installs over
v0.2.0 without uninstalling, and the bridge stays paired.

**If you build a bridge: feed an E220-900T30 from 5 V**, not 3V3, or it transmits 20-30 dB
below its setting. See `docs/BUILDING.md`.

### What changed since v0.2.0

- A new status screen. Its first line says whether positions are flowing, and if not, what to
  do. Below it: the riders heard, by name, with signal bars, how long ago and how much of their
  traffic got through; then the bridge and its transmit power. Checks, Log and Settings are tabs.
  On a phone held sideways or a tablet, the status sits beside them. Light and dark follow the
  phone.
- Checks lists only what is wrong, each with a button to put it right, or says "All good". The
  phone's GPS and its clock are checked each time the app starts. A missing DMD2 is named.
- Transmit power is Low, Med, High or Max. A tap now reaches the bridge at once. Before, a second
  tap could wait up to 35 s, holding positions and telling DMD2 the radio was gone.
- The notification uses the app's words and shows the state as a coloured badge: green, amber,
  red. The app has its own icon, and a lost bridge shows a crossed-out pin in the status bar.
- Autostart is a switch in Settings. The test tools are hidden until shown.
- The log on screen is lighter: the bridge's status appears when it changes. Share log still
  sends everything. The screen no longer redraws itself in the background.

### Notes

- After the app is updated or restarted, DMD2 may need the group shared again before the bridge
  transmits. The app says so: "Reshare the group in DMD2".
- Transmit power: in the EU, 868.0-868.6 MHz allows 25 mW ERP, about 14 dBm, and every step of
  an E220-900T30 is above that.
