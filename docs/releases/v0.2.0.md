**Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by DMD Navigation / Thork Racing.**
Experimental, and ridden: positions shared with stock DMD2 devices, a NOR7E included, with no
network, on group rides from 27 September to 2 October 2026.

Sideload the APK (it replaces the genuine DMD-LoRa app; they cannot coexist), and flash the image
for your board with `esptool.py write_flash 0x0 <file>`. Both halves come from the same commit and
must match. Flashing the full image clears the bridge's pairing, so pair it again in the app
afterwards.

**Before you ride: feed an E220-900T30 from 5 V.** Wire its VCC to the board's 5V/VIN pin, not
3V3. On 3V3 it transmitted 20-30 dB below its setting, the likely reason a tablet kept losing one
of our bridges on the 2 October ride. The 22 dBm E22 is fine on 3V3. See `docs/BUILDING.md`.

### What changed since v0.1.0

- One firmware image for both EBYTE parts, E220 and E22: the module says which it is.
- Interop with the stock app: only the sender is marked direct, transmissions are spaced by group
  size as the stock app does, our packets go out with TTL 1 so stock devices no longer relay them,
  and stock relays are read.
- Clocks: every position we pass on carries an age our app can vouch for on its own clock - our
  own aged from the fix itself, retold ones no fresher than the evidence - so a device with a
  wrong clock no longer spreads frozen riders through ours. Time check and GPS check in Tools show
  a wrong clock.
- Pacing on no fixed beat, so two of our devices no longer fall into step and deafen each other.
  Past nine riders our own position goes first.
- Chat lines are sent three times, since nothing acknowledges them. The whole ride's log is kept
  in a file, with Share log.
- The notification and the status panel name a missing radio, a silent DMD2, a stale GPS fix and
  a quiet channel, and an alert fires when the bridge drops right after power-up.
- Bridge firmware: a watchdog and a Bluetooth supervisor, the reset reason in its status, a module
  probe that rides out a busy moment, a confirmation for every transmission, and the signal
  strength of every received packet, shown in the app.

### Notes

- Transmit power: in the EU, 868.0-868.6 MHz allows 25 mW ERP, about 14 dBm, and every step of an
  E220-900T30 is above that. On a T30 prefer level 2 to level 3: on the bench the weakest step
  occasionally lost packets at a distant receiver.
- SX1262 boards (Heltec V3, T-Deck) now send EBYTE's sync word, and EBYTE modules take their
  packets (38-40 of 40 on the bench). They are not in this release: their real transmit power is
  not measured yet.
